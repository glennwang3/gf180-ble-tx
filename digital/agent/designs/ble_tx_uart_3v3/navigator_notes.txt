This is ble_tx_uart (BLE modulator + UART receiver, one flattened netlist, seven
ports) hardened for a 3.3 V supply: the corners are tt_025C_3v30, ss_125C_3v00
and ff_n40C_3v60, each with nominal, minimum and maximum parasitics. Its RTL is
clock-gated (44 integrated clock gates: an idle gate, a symbol-rate gate, one gate
in front of the 39 per-byte PDU gates, and UART register gates), which cut its
power about 4x. About 50,000 um2 of cells after synthesis.

At 3.0 V the slow corner is where layouts fail: cells are much weaker and the
3 ns max-slew limit is hard to meet. The fixed settings in config.yaml are what
made it close (weak 1x cells excluded, repair after global routing, detailed
placement allowed to move cells 300 um vertically, 0.25 ns hold margin). The
baseline n0 is 285 um wide and signed off with no violation of any kind at any
of the 9 corners: about 69,400 um2 of cells at 91% utilisation. A manual sweep
with the same settings found: 290 um clean, 285 um clean, 280 um left 9 slew
violations at ss, 270 um left 253 (97% full), and placement density 90 at
280 um made it worse (381 slew violations and a hold miss). Widths below 280 um
are very unlikely to close; 280 um is the realistic target for die area.

Nothing may be violated at signoff, at any of the 9 corners: setup, hold, max
slew, max capacitance, max fanout, DRC, LVS, antenna, XOR. The gate rejects a
result with any of them. In-loop estimates miss most slow-corner slew problems,
so evaluate promising results rather than trusting their estimates. Keep
MAX_FANOUT_CONSTRAINT at 8 or above (clock buffers drive up to 8 sinks).

Power carries real weight here. It is measured on each result's own netlist from
a simulated packet loaded over the UART pin plus 1 ms of idle after it, because a
beacon spends almost all its time idle. Most of the idle power is the clock tree
(root and main buffers, CTS delay buffers and dummy loads) and the free-running
UART flops, so the clock-tree settings are the main power lever.
